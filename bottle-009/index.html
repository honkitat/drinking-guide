BACKGROUND

Clos des Goisses is a 5.83-hectare walled vineyard in Mareuil-sur-Aÿ, planted on a 45° slope that drops toward the Marne — the steepest vineyard in Champagne, and one of the oldest clos in the region. Philipponnat has owned it for nearly a century, and it is the only Champagne house whose flagship is a single walled plot rather than a blend of villages.

2003 was the heatwave year: sugar without much acid, and a Champagne that came out broad, honeyed and Pinot-driven rather than sharp. Roughly two-thirds Pinot Noir to one-third Chardonnay here, which is why it reads more like a white Burgundy with bubbles than an aperitif.
