BACKGROUND

Clase Azul started in Guadalajara in 1997, when Arturo Lomelí bottled tequila in hand-made ceramic rather than glass. Each decanter is shaped and painted by Mazahua artisans in Santa María Canchesdá and takes about two weeks to finish — which is why the empties end up on shelves instead of in the recycling.

The agave is 100% blue Weber from Los Altos de Jalisco, slow-cooked before fermentation; this reposado rests eight months in American whiskey casks — enough for vanilla and hazelnut, not enough to bury the cooked agave.

TASTING NOTES

Pale gold; medium to full body. Cooked agave and agave nectar lead, with candied orange peel, toasted wood and a subtle hazelnut note from the American whiskey casks.
