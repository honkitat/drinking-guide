BACKGROUND

Faiveley has been a négociant house in Nuits-Saint-Georges since 1825, and it is now in its seventh generation with Erwan Faiveley running it. Clos de Bèze is the other great grand cru of Gevrey-Chambertin, 15.4 hectares lying above Chambertin itself, and the two have been argued over for two centuries as to which is finer.

The label is unusually honest: 3,022 bottles from this harvest, this one No. 0636, bottled at the domaine. 2015 was a warm, concentrated vintage in the Côte de Nuits, and Clos de Bèze carried it well — rose and dark cherry over a firm, savoury spine.
