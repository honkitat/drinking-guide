BACKGROUND

The Carillon name has farmed Puligny for roughly four centuries — “1611” is the date they print on the label. The domaine itself dates from 2010, when Louis Carillon retired and the estate split amicably between his sons François and Jacques; both labels still carry the same weight in the village.

Les Folatières is Puligny's largest premier cru: a steep east-to-south-east slope at about 276 m, thin soil over limestone scree. It ripens early, so the wines come out broader than the village's tightest premiers crus — stone fruit and honeysuckle rather than the razor edge of a Pucelles.

2023 was Burgundy's warmest year on record. The whites came in generous in fruit and volume, the risk being low acid and high alcohol where picking ran late; the best kept a racy line under all that richness.
