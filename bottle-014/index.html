BACKGROUND

Mitobe Shuzo has brewed in Tendo since 1898 and is one of the few Yamagata breweries fermenting with hard water (around 120 hardness), which gives 山形正宗 its reputation for a sharp, sword-like finish. Everything has been junmai since 2010, pressed slowly in wooden fune boxes, with some 850 ares of the family's own rice.

Kamenoo is the Meiji-era Yamagata landrace from which much of eastern Japan's rice descends, used here alone at 50% polish. It is a limited seasonal bottling rather than the year-round Omachi junmai ginjo.
