BACKGROUND

Fukuchiyo Shuzo was founded in 1923 on the Ariake Sea coast at Kashima in Saga and launched 鍋島 in 1998; its Champion Sake award at the IWC in 2011 turned a small Kyushu brewery into one of Japan's most wanted labels. The house works by matching individual rice varieties to their own expressions rather than one fixed profile.

This bottling takes Yamadanishiki from Yoshikawa in Hyogo, a special-A district, mills it to 45% and ferments to 16%. At ¥3,680 per 720 ml in Japan it sits mid-tier in the premium line — above the junmai ginjo, below the 35% daiginjo and the shizuku drips.
