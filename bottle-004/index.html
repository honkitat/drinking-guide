BACKGROUND

Ferrari was founded in Trento in 1902 and is Italy's reference traditional-method sparkling house; the label still says “dal 1902”. Riserva Lunelli is the family's own cuvée — 100% Chardonnay from mountain vineyards, picked by hand, and the only Ferrari whose first fermentation happens in large Austrian oak casks, the way it was done in Bruno Lunelli's time.

It rests a minimum of 69 months on the lees, so a 2016 is only now arriving in condition. Expect gold, smoke and crème brûlée over ripe apricot, with mountain acidity holding the richness in place — Italy's answer to Champagne, from a colder, higher place.
